## Risk Summary

The S11 cutover originally concentrated unrelated responsibilities in a small number of dense helpers while iterating on external provider wake/resume behavior. The risky shapes were functions that both inspected runtime state and decided delivery outcomes, or parsed protocol markers and mapped delivery confirmation in the same body. Those are genuine `MULTI-CLASSIFIER-RISK` surfaces under `/home/nes/ai/conventions/code-quality.md` because they mix categories such as accessor, parser, validator, predicate, mapper, formatter, and orchestration.
